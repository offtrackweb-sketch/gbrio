About G-Brio

Iconic heritage, modern freedom.

Who we are

The original G‑Class, restored by hand and built to your specification.

G-Brio unites the off-road heritage of the Mercedes-Benz G-Class with the ease of an open-top car. Every vehicle starts as an original W460 or W461 and is fully restored — body, frame, drivetrain and interior — before it is finished to the specification you define.

We build one car at a time, never in series. From first configuration to delivery at your address, you deal with the same small team throughout.

What we do

Three disciplines. One vehicle.

Restoration

Complete frame-off restoration of the original G-Class. Body, chassis, drivetrain and electrics are rebuilt to as-new condition using original Mercedes-Benz parts.

Conversion

The open-top conversion, interior trim and technical upgrades — engine, gearbox, comfort and off-road equipment — are carried out in-house, by hand.

Configuration

Paint, soft top, leather, stitching, rims and equipment are specified by you in our 3D configurator. Every G-Brio leaves the workshop as a one-off.

Our standard

Original substance. Modern reliability.

A G-Brio is not a replica. It is an original G-Class with its character intact — rebuilt to a standard that makes it dependable for everyday use, and backed by a full warranty.

  • Original Mercedes-Benz G-Class, W460 / W461
  • Full frame-off restoration by hand
  • Original parts and documented history
  • Full warranty, service at Mercedes-Benz partners worldwide

The process

From configuration to delivery.

Six stages. One point of contact throughout.

  1. 01

    Configuration

    Specify your G-Brio in the 3D configurator — model, engine, paint, interior and equipment — and send us the result.

  2. 02

    Consultation & order

    We review your configuration together, refine details where needed and confirm the order.

  3. 03

    Restoration & conversion

    Your vehicle is fully restored and converted by hand — body, frame, drivetrain and interior, to your specification.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    We ship your G-Brio safely to the address of your choice, anywhere in the world.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Your digital quick-start guide covers functions, operation and care — available at any time.

  6. 06

    Warranty & service

    Every G-Brio comes with a full warranty. Maintenance is possible at Mercedes-Benz partners worldwide.
